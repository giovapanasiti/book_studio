# Spring Tides

The spring tides came in March with their old arithmetic:

1. The moon pulls the water
2. The water pulls the boats
3. The boats pull the sailors, who cannot help themselves

She recorded the levels in a table she had ruled by hand. The columns leaned slightly to the left, like everything she wrote.

![Tide movements](images/tide-charts.png)

The village below sent up bread on Thursdays, and questions all week. *When is the storm coming?* — as if the light could see forward in time as well as twenty miles out.

---

On the last day of the tides she found a message bottle, empty. She put a message in and threw it back. Fair is fair.
