# The Keeper

The lighthouse at Punta Serena had kept its light for a **hundred and nine years**, and Elena Ferrán had kept the lighthouse for eleven of them. She knew the *taste* of the fog before it arrived and the particular silence the gulls kept before a storm.

Every morning she climbed the ninety-two steps with her thermos and her notebook, and every morning the sea looked like a different animal.

## The Logbook

Her predecessors had left their marks in the logbook:

- *1931* — "Light steady. Two ships. One whale, probably."
- *1954* — "Storm took the door. Door recovered."
- *1977* — "Nothing happened. Perfect day."

> A lighthouse is not a building, her grandfather had written. It is an argument with the dark, renewed nightly.

She intended to win the argument for another decade at least.
