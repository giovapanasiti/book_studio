# The Glass

The first-order Fresnel lens was the one true treasure of Punta Serena. Cleaning it took a full day and a specific vocabulary:

```
06:00  shutters closed, lamp cold
08:15  prisms, north face — chamois only
11:40  brass ring, rouge polish
15:00  alignment check against the sun-line
```

The glassmakers of the old workshops had ground each prism by hand. **Light bends politely for good glass**, her teacher used to say, *and rudely for bad.*
